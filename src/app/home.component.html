Welcome to Cuckoo Colors

Color coordinate activities

Cuckoo Colors is your destination for generating custom color coordinate activities that balance creativity, logic, and accessibility.

Get Started

What is this?

Cuckoo Colors makes printable activity sheets for vision therapy. Each sheet has a grid of cells, like a spreadsheet, with columns labeled A, B, C and rows numbered 1, 2, 3. You paint a pattern in the grid, and the tool writes a list of coordinates for each color, for example "Red: A1, B3, C2". A person then recreates the pattern on a blank grid from that list. Finding each cell, matching it to a color and coloring it in works on visual tracking, scanning and attention.

Why it exists

Therapists and parents often build these sheets by hand, which is slow and easy to get wrong. This tool generates a new one in seconds. It was built to be accessible: every color is shown with its name as well as its swatch, the page works with a keyboard and screen reader, and the printout is plain and high contrast.

How to use it

  1. Open Color Coordinate and choose the number of rows, columns and colors.
  2. Press Generate. Table 1 lists your colors and, as you paint, the cells that belong to each. Change a color with its drop-down.
  3. Table 2 is the grid. Choose a color in Table 1, then click cells to paint your pattern.
  4. Press Print to get a sheet with the coordinate list and the grid.

The colors come from a shared list on the Color Management page. You can suggest new colors there or from the generator.
